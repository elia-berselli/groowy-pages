The goal is not to record every cent forever. It is to answer a practical question: *what changed this month, and what do I want to do differently next month?*

## Start with a question, not the perfect category system

At first, a small set of categories is enough when it separates real choices: home, daily essentials, transport, health, leisure and recurring commitments. If a category does not help you make a choice, it is probably too detailed. Tags and notes are useful later, when you need to find a specific case without rebuilding your system every week.

## Keep the routine short enough to repeat

1. Record income and spending as it happens, or collect it for a short review.
2. Check amount, date and category before saving: a reliable base is worth more than quick automation.
3. At the end of the period, look only at what can change a decision: subscriptions, repeated costs, unexpected spikes or delayed goals.

Groowy is designed to bring movements, categories, tags and recurring items into a readable personal record. A scan or suggestion may speed up entry, but it is never an unchecked truth.

## Observation is not judgement

A monthly total does not say whether spending was “good” or “bad”. It gives you something concrete to discuss with yourself or someone sharing a spending project. That is why it helps to keep your personal record separate first, then decide what should be shared.
