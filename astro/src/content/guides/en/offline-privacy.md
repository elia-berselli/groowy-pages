“Offline-first” does not mean that no data can ever leave a phone. It means the personal record starts on the device and does not need a connected bank account to provide a useful base. It is a starting point, not an absolute slogan.

## Separate the local base from optional services

Cloud, backup, groups, AI services and future connections each have a different purpose. Before enabling them, ask the same question: *which data is needed, where does it go, for how long and how do I stop?* An external feature should not become a silent requirement for using a personal record.

## A scan does not remove your control

When an assistive feature proposes fields from a document, the result needs review before it is saved. Payment information has an even tighter boundary: no full PAN, CVC/CVV, PIN, front/back image or raw OCR as an archive. Read [Security and data](/en/security/) for the verified perimeter and the [full privacy notice](/privacy/) for the authoritative legal text.

## Banking connections are not available today

Possible read-only AIS access is a roadmap direction, not a connected provider or announced partnership. If and when it is evaluated, consent, revocation, minimisation and a regulated provider must be verified before a pilot. The [AIS partner page](/en/partners/open-banking/) clearly separates that direction from the available product.
